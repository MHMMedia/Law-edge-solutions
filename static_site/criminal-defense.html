The buyer is in a hurry

Defense isn't a slow sale. It's a same-day decision.

A DUI or arrest sends someone, or a panicked spouse, to Google within hours, usually from a phone, usually after midnight.

01: THE CLOCK

Utah deadlines compress the decision

After a Utah DUI arrest, the officer takes the license and hands over a yellow Notice of Suspension that drives for 29 days. The driver has just 10 days to demand a DLD hearing or the suspension is automatic. Arraignment can land within 72 hours. Nobody shops around for two weeks.

02: THE SEARCH

Nights and weekends, on a phone

DUI searches spike from Friday evening through early Monday. The queries are blunt: "DUI lawyer tonight," "first offense DUI attorney Salt Lake City." If your firm isn't there on mobile, in that window, the case goes to whoever is.

03: THE FIELD

Everyone is bidding on the same words

"DUI lawyer near me" is one of the priciest clicks in legal, $40 to $100 in competitive metros. Burning that budget only works if your intake actually answers. Most firms don't.

Speed to lead

Be first, be visible, answer in minutes

A lead contacted within five minutes is roughly 21x likelier to convert than one called back a half hour later. The average firm takes eight hours. That gap is the whole game.
