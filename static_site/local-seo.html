The 3-Pack Is the Whole Game

Three spots. Most of the clicks.

Google's local results show three firms above the fold. The map pack outranks the blue links below it for a "lawyer near me" search, so that's where we fight.

01: RELEVANCE

Match the search

Google ranks the map pack on relevance, distance, and prominence. Relevance starts with your primary category, the single strongest lever you control. "Personal injury attorney" and "law firm" rank for very different searches. Why it matters: The wrong primary category buries you for the exact search your best client just typed.

02: DISTANCE

Own your radius

Proximity to the searcher matters, so one office in Salt Lake won't rank you in Provo or Ogden by default. We build location pages and service-area signals so you show up across the Wasatch Front, not just your block. Why it matters: Distance to the searcher is a real ranking factor, without service-area signals, one office only ranks one neighborhood.

03: PROMINENCE

Earn the trust signals

Prominence is reviews, citations, links, and mentions across the web. It's the slow part. It's also the part competitors can't copy overnight, which is exactly why it's worth doing right. Why it matters: Reviews, citations and links are slow trust signals, but once earned, they are the moat competitors cannot buy overnight.

Google Business Profile

What makes a Google Business Profile rank?

Your website doesn't show in the map pack. Your Business Profile does. We make every field earn its place.

Categories & services

Right primary category, plus secondaries for each practice area. Services and attributes filled out so Google knows exactly what you do. Why it matters: A complete services list is how Google matches you to the long-tail search a ready-to-hire client actually types.

Address & service area

Google requires a real, staffed address for a storefront listing, no virtual offices. We set it up correctly so your firm isn't suspended or filtered. Why it matters: A virtual-office listing gets suspended, a real, staffed address keeps you in the pack.

Posts & Q&A

Weekly posts, real photos, and seeded Q&A keep the profile active. A stale listing reads as a closed firm to both Google and the client. Why it matters: Fresh posts and photos tell Google the firm is active, stale profiles rank lower.

Reviews, Done Ethically

How should a law firm ask for reviews?

Reviews move rankings and they move clients. We build a system to ask the right way, within the your state bar (Utah State Bar shown as an example)'s advertising rules.

Google weighs review count, average rating, and how recent they are. A firm with steady five-star reviews from the last 30 days beats a firm with more reviews that all stopped two years ago. So we don't chase a one-time pile of reviews. We build a quiet intake habit: a review request that goes out at the right moment, to the right client, with a direct link to your profile.

ASK

At the right moment

We trigger the request after a resolved matter or a five-star moment, by text or email, wired into your CRM so nobody has to remember. Why it matters: Timing the ask to a five-star moment is what actually earns the review, not a generic follow-up.

RESPOND

To every review

You reply to all of them, good and bad, without ever confirming someone was a client. We keep responses compliant and on-brand. Why it matters: Every reply is public, a compliant answer builds trust without ever confirming someone was a client.

NEVER

Buy or gate them

No purchased reviews, no review-gating, no incentives. That's a Google violation and a bar-discipline risk. Real reviews or none. Why it matters: Buying or gating reviews risks a Google penalty and a bar complaint, one violation erases months of trust.

Citations & NAP Consistency

Why must your name, address, and phone match everywhere?

Google cross-checks your firm's details across the web. Conflicting listings erode the trust your ranking depends on.

NAP is name, address, phone. When your office shows as "Suite 200" on Avvo, "Ste. 200" on Justia, and an old phone number on a directory you forgot about, Google gets a muddier picture of who you are, and that costs you in the pack. We audit every citation, fix the conflicts, and lock in one consistent record.

Legal directories

Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Lawyers.com, and the your state bar (Utah State Bar shown as an example) listing, claimed, corrected, and pointed at one NAP. Why it matters: Legal directories are where Google cross-checks your firm, one mismatch muddies the whole record.

Local & data aggregators

Chamber and Wasatch Front local directories, plus the aggregators that feed maps and voice search across the 801. Why it matters: Data aggregators feed hundreds of maps and directories, fix it once there, fix it everywhere.
