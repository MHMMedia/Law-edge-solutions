Two Ways to Win the Top of Google

Google Ads and Local Services Ads, run together

Search ads catch the high-intent keyword. Local Services Ads sit above them and charge you per lead. We bid on both so your firm owns the space competitors fight over.

01: LOCAL SERVICES ADS

Pay per lead, not per click

Local Services Ads (LSAs) run at the very top of the page and bill only when someone calls or messages you. For attorneys, leads typically land in the $75–$300 range depending on practice area and city (representative figures). No charge for a curious click that goes nowhere. Why it matters: You only pay when a real prospect reaches out, the waste clicks never hit your card.

02: GOOGLE SEARCH ADS

Own the keyword that signs cases

"Personal injury lawyer" clears $150 a click in many markets, and Salt Lake long-tails like "Provo truck accident attorney" run higher. We tighten match types, negatives, and landing pages so you pay for buyers, not browsers. Why it matters: Without negatives and tight matches, broad terms burn budget on browsers who will never hire you.

03: GOOGLE SCREENED

The green check that earns trust

To run Local Services Ads (LSAs), your firm passes a background check, bar-license verification, and an insurance review to earn the Google Screened badge. That badge sits next to your name when a stranger is choosing who to call first. Why it matters: In a list of strangers, the verified check is the first trust signal, it decides who gets the first call.

Know Your Real Cost Per Case

Call tracking ties spend to signed clients

Most firms can't tell you what a case actually costs to acquire. We can, down to the keyword that made the phone ring.

TRACK

A number for every source

We set up call tracking (CallRail or similar) with dynamic number insertion. When someone clicks your ad, the phone number on the page swaps to a unique tracking line, so the call ties back to the exact campaign and search term. Why it matters: Without a unique number per source, you are guessing which ad actually made the phone ring.

MEASURE

Cost per acquisition, by practice area

A new DUI client and a wrongful-death client don't cost the same to win. Call tracking exposes your true cost per acquisition per practice area, so we shift budget toward the campaigns that close, and cut the ones that don't. Why it matters: Not all practice areas cost the same to win, knowing the real cost per case tells you where to spend and where to stop.

REPORT

Reporting you can actually read

You see calls, qualified leads, and cost per case in plain language. Recorded calls let us grade lead quality and tune intake, not just ad copy. No vanity dashboards. Why it matters: Reports on clicks lie, recordings tell you if the lead was actually hireable.

When Paid Search Is the Right Move

PPC vs. SEO, and why most firms need both

Paid search buys position today. SEO earns it over months. The mistake is treating them as either/or.

RUN PPC WHEN

You need cases now

New office, a slow month, or a practice area you're trying to grow this quarter, PPC and Local Services Ads (LSAs) put you at the top of Salt Lake search results immediately. You control the spigot. Turn it up before a hiring push, dial it back when your calendar fills. Why it matters: Paid search is the only channel where you control the lead volume dial week to week.

LEAN SEO WHEN

You want durable, cheaper traffic

Once those legal CPCs add up, every click stops the moment you stop paying. Our law firm SEO work builds rankings that keep producing leads after the ad budget pauses. It's slower, and it compounds.

DO BOTH WHEN

You're serious about market share

Paid covers you while SEO matures, then SEO lowers your blended cost per case as it ranks. Firms that run both own more of the page, map pack, organic, and ads. Why it matters: Owning two or three spots on page one pushes competitors below the fold and doubles your chance of the call. See how that plays out on our results page and transparent pricing.
