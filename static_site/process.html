The Three Phases

Audit, build, grow

No mystery, no jargon. Every engagement moves through the same three phases, in the same order.

01: AUDIT

Find the leaks first

Before we touch anything, we pull apart your Google Business Profile, site speed, rankings, and intake. Most firms are bleeding leads somewhere obvious, a buried phone number, a slow page, calls that go to voicemail at 5:30. We tell you what we find, even the unflattering parts.

02: BUILD

Fix the foundation

We rebuild what's costing you cases: practice-area pages that actually rank, a site that loads fast on a phone, conversion paths that turn a click into a booked consult. This is the unglamorous work that makes everything after it pay off.

03: GROW

Turn it up

Once the foundation holds, we scale what's working: SEO, Google Ads, and Local Services Ads running together so you get cases now and durable rankings later. We double down on the channels that book consults and cut the ones that don't.

Onboarding

What the first 90 days look like

Marketing isn't a light switch. Here's a realistic timeline so you know what to expect, and when to expect it.

DAYS 1–14

Access & audit

We request access to your accounts and run a full baseline audit: rankings, site health, competitors, and how your intake actually handles a 9 p.m. call. You'll know your starting line.

DAYS 15–30

Strategy & first moves

You get a documented 90-day plan with KPI targets, and the first work goes live, quick wins on your Google Business Profile and the pages costing you the most. Local Services Ads setup begins here; bar verification can take a few weeks, so we start early.

DAYS 31–90

Build & calibrate

Full execution. New pages ship, ads collect real data, and we tune toward cost per signed case. SEO is the slow burn, most firms see meaningful organic movement around the 3–6 month mark, so this window sets it up.
