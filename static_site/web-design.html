Speed & accessibility

Fast pages that everyone can use

Google measures real-user experience, and so do your future clients. We build to both.

CORE WEB VITALS

Built to pass

We target Google's thresholds at the 75th percentile of real visits: Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5s, Interaction to Next Paint under 200ms, layout shift under 0.1. Slow sites bleed clients, Google found 53% of mobile visitors leave a page that takes over three seconds to load. Why it matters: Google ranks what users experience — fast pages keep visitors and rankings.

ADA / WCAG

Accessible by default

We build to WCAG 2.1 AA, the standard courts lean on in ADA Title III cases. Web-accessibility lawsuits have climbed for years, and law firms are not exempt. An inaccessible site shuts out clients and invites a demand letter. Why it matters: Accessibility keeps you open to every client and lowers legal risk.

MOBILE

Phone-first

Most legal searches happen on a phone, often right after an accident or arrest. Tap targets, click-to-call, and a form that's easy to finish one-handed come standard. Why it matters: If it is not effortless on a phone, that client calls the next firm.
